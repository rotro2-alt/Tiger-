Notes from the archive.

How do I find my fit?

The size on each piece is a sample label. Real listings will include garment measurements, so compare them with a favourite item you already own.

What does the condition mean?

"Very good" and "Good" are illustrative in this demo. Real listings will describe wear, repairs and visible flaws, with close-up photos.

Can I save pieces for later?

Tap the heart on a piece to save it. Favourites stay in this browser on this device. Saving does not reserve an item.

How do I order?

Browse the archive, save the pieces you love, and reach out when the full collection launches. No payment is taken on this site.
